tynet-cloud-init installed in {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Watch it come up:

  microk8s kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pod,svc,certificate

Smoke test (bypasses DNS, resolves straight to the MetalLB VIP):

  curl -sS --resolve cloud-init.tynet.us:443:{{ .Values.service.loadBalancerIP }} \
    https://cloud-init.tynet.us/healthcheck

Cert rotation does not hot-reload — main.go loads tls.crt/tls.key once at
startup. After a renewal, restart to pick it up:

  microk8s kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} rollout restart deployment/tynet-cloud-init
